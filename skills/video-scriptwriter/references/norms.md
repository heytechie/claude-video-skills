# Norms from research (2026-10)

## 22 top tech-brand launch/feature videos (Linear, Raycast, Vercel, Notion, Figma, Dia, Framer, Apple, Stripe, Cursor, Claude, OpenAI, Superhuman, Granola, Perplexity, Lovable, HeyGen)
- Length: median ~52 s (range 20–90). Teasers 30–40 s; single-feature films 45–70 s; flagships 78–90 s.
- Time to product: median 2.5 s across software videos (9 of 17 within 2.5 s). Story spots hold it to 45–80 % of runtime.
- Mode: ~55 % text-only + music, ~23 % VO/founder, ~23 % live-action story.
- Words per card: median 3–4 (range 1–7); taglines up to ~9; longer lines only when built word by word.
- Holds: 1–3-word list cards 0.5–0.8 s; 4–7-word headlines 1.5–2 s incl. type-on; end tagline 2–3 s; logo 2–6 s. Effective reading budget ~3–3.5 wps.
- VO: 2.3–2.9 wps. Story spots 0.4–1.6 wps (silence as timing).
- Demo beats 8–15 s each in long films; 30 s fits one feature, 60–90 s fits 2–4.
- First typed prompt 6–25 concrete words; follow-ups 5–12.
- CTA 1–5 words; end sequence (tagline + CTA + logo) 4–8 s (~10 % of runtime). Only ~1/3 show a URL; others end on availability, a question, a command, or a tagline.
- Recurring devices: the product's own text as the script (prompts, status verbs, toasts); escalating loops (each cycle adds a capability, ends on a scale shot); split-sentence cards; typography styled with the feature itself; a human/mascot reaction line as payoff; proof via chart, live counter, community output, or "made with the product".

## Platform research
- Mobile feed attention per post ≈ 1.7 s; motion and promise in the first second.
- Pitch by 3 s; brand/product by 5 s (Google ABCD; Kantar-linked study of 11k ads: +30 % short-term sales likelihood when followed).
- Facebook: ~47 % of campaign value delivered in first 3 s, ~74 % by 10 s; captions add ~12 % view time.
- LinkedIn: ~79 % of feed video viewed muted; single audio source improves completion; offers ~1.2× completion.
- Silent reading ~238 wpm (~4 wps) average; text-only cards budget ≤ 3 wps.
- Wistia: < 1 min videos ~52 % engagement; Vidyard: ~66 % finish business videos under 60 s.
- Unverified and not to be cited: "85 % watch muted", "71 % decide in 3 s".

## Known conflicts
- Brand early (platforms) vs logo-first skipped (ad strategists) → product/brand colour early, logo at the end.
- Fast cuts (1.7 s) vs "slow down" (retention consultants) → fast for demo beats, longer holds for text.
- Short (≤ 15 s guidance) vs viral long launches (45–110 s) → paid/feature = short; organic flagship = narrative, plus cutdown.
- Polish vs "ugly wins" → clarity first; production must never upstage the product.
