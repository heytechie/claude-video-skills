# Selling launch videos (proposal template)

Lead filters (Upwork-style): payment verified · $10K+ spent · hire rate > 50 % · posted < 30 min ago · < 15 proposals, nobody interviewing yet · company recently funded / about to launch. Search variants: "SaaS launch video", "product launch video", "UI animation", "app promo video", "SaaS explainer".

Structure: **their task → your concept for *their* product → small first milestone → clear deadline.** The first two lines are all they see in the preview, so make them about the client and use the exact keywords from the post.

```
[Product] needs a [length] launch video that shows [their core feature, in their words] before [launch date].

Concept: open on [their user's pain, one image], [product] arrives as [the turn], and we show [their 1–2 must-show features] working on your real Figma UI, ending on [their CTA]. Crisp vector UI, 1080p/60, plus 9:16 and 1:1 cutdowns.

First milestone: a scene-by-scene storyboard within 24 h. If you like the direction, the full video follows in [1–2] days, revisions same day.

[Name] · [link to 1–2 samples or a 10 s spec piece made for them]
```

Positioning: speed (next-day delivery), same-hour revisions (one-line changes), and reliability. Aim to become the client's ongoing motion partner (launch → feature updates → ads → next launch). Quote the value, not the hours.
